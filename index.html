01 Skill & source

Select the base of your equipped boots. Values use the supplied S14 compiled Armor.bin; unique and set names use their underlying boot base. Choose Custom to enter another range.

ED includes active auras and buffs when the explosion is rolled. Exclude strength (added separately), local weapon ED and charge-only bonuses to the initial hit. Tiger Strike transfer to the rebuilt explosion is not assumed.

Flat damage & kick bonuses

Use effective active-hand totals. Weapon base physical damage is excluded. Boot base damage is added before ED; the supplied boots use a strength coefficient of 100. The native worn-boot helper adds the kick-damage stat twice.

Enter the physical seed used by the explosion, before Critical/Deadly Strike and enemy resistance. This is not the character sheet’s already-scaled Dragon Tail fire damage.

02 Critical & Deadly Strike

Base multipliers: Critical 2×; Deadly 1.5×. Critical is checked first (75% cap); Deadly is checked if it fails (75% + maximum DS). Chance is separate from multiplier.

03 The monster receiving the explosion

Ordinary pierce cannot break immunity. The model halves breaking reductions against initial immunity, rounds each source separately, halves the below-zero reduction portion and caps the result at −100%. Debuffs must be effective on this monster; no automatic boss curse scaling.

04 Optional sustained estimate

Enter an observed attack cycle; 12 is an example, not an IAS breakpoint. Charges, movement, downtime, block/dodge rules and other skill releases are not simulated.

Timing & boundaries

  • The area damage runs during the kick’s server action. No separate explosion timer was found.
  • The visual missile’s 16-frame lifetime is not a 16-frame damage delay.
  • The burst is centred on the kicked target, with a radius parameter of 6. It is not a travelling ring of damaging missiles.
  • The target’s physical resistance does not reduce the fire seed.
  • The initial kick and other charge releases are not added to these fire-only results.
